# Registration Data Retention Draft

Status: Requires owner and school approval before production launch.

- Active registration data is retained through workshop administration and attendance reconciliation.
- Guardian consent evidence is retained only as long as required for safeguarding and audit.
- Email delivery metadata excludes message body content where possible.
- Rate limit hashes expire after 15 minutes and do not contain raw IP addresses.
- Marketing consent is separate from event administration.
- A post-event review should occur within 30 days.
- Records without a continuing operational or safeguarding purpose should be deleted or minimised within 90 days after the event.
- Audit records must not include full email content, secrets, or unnecessary personal data.
- Access, correction, and deletion requests must be tracked and completed through an approved contact process.

## Request handling procedure

1. Verify the requester through the email address on the registration record or an approved guardian channel.
2. Record the request date, requester, requested action, and the organizer who handled it without copying unnecessary registration details into the audit note.
3. Use the private organizer export only to locate the record. Do not send a full export to satisfy an individual request.
4. Correct or delete the D1 registration, related guardian consent, and queued email metadata only after the responsible organizer approves the action.
5. Confirm completion through the approved communication channel and record the completion date.
6. Do not delete records that must temporarily be retained for safeguarding, legal, or active workshop administration. Document the reason and review date instead.
